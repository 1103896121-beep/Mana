# Mana - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 18, 2026

## Introduction
Mana ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Mana mobile application (the "App").

## Data Collection and Storage
Mana is designed with a privacy-first approach. 
1. **Local Storage:** All your focus data, task history, and anonymous preferences are encrypted and stored **entirely and exclusively on your local device**.
2. **No Cloud Syncing:** We do not automatically upload, sync, or back up any of your personal data to our servers or any third-party clouds.
3. **No Tracking:** We do not collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or usage analytics without your explicit consent.

## Third-Party Services
The App may utilize third-party services (such as Apple's In-App Purchase system) solely for the purpose of facilitating optional support or donations. These services have their own privacy policies, and we do not have access to your personal financial information.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy here and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Support page or via the App.

# Mana - 隐私协议

**生效日期：** 2026年3月18日

## 引言
Mana（“我们”）深知隐私对您的重要性。本隐私协议解释了当您使用 Mana 移动应用程序（“应用”）时，我们在收集、使用和披露信息方面的做法。

## 数据收集与存储
Mana 的核心设计理念是“隐私优先”：
1. **本地存储：** 您的所有专注数据、任务历史记录和匿名偏好设置，**完全且仅加密存储在您的本机设备上**。
2. **无云端同步：** 我们绝无任何未经授权的云端同步行为，绝不会暗中将您的任何个人数据上传、备份到我们的服务器或任何第三方云端。
3. **无追踪：** 在未经您明确授权的情况下，我们不会收集、追踪或共享您的任何个人身份信息 (PII) 或使用分析数据。

## 第三方服务
本应用可能会使用第三方服务（如 Apple 的应用内购买系统，In-App Purchase），但仅限于为您提供可选的赞助支持功能。这些服务受其各自的隐私政策约束，我们无法且不会获取您的个人财务信息。

## 协议的更改
我们可能会不时更新我们的隐私协议。如果有任何修改，我们将在此处发布新的隐私协议并更新顶部的“生效日期”。

## 联系我们
如果您对我们的隐私协议有任何疑问或建议，请随时通过我们的支持页面或应用内的相关渠道联系我们。
